name: Programme scope
description: Standing product requirements and source-document boundaries for Adhyayaan.
---
Adhyayaan serves college students completing final-year internships of 60 or 120 hours, with hands-on projects and evaluation. The user explicitly requested custom authentication and said attractive UI is non-negotiable.

**Why:** These are explicit user requirements.

**How to apply:** Preserve the custom-authentication choice and visual quality when extending this product. Source-material caveats and operational boundaries are documented in `replit.md`.

The signed-out main homepage should be an attractive, intuitive login/sign-up experience rather than showing learning progress. Keep the student's actual progress behind sign-in.

**Why:** The user requested separate login/sign-up pages after seeing progress on the public homepage.

**How to apply:** Keep authentication pages distinct from the learning workspace; do not show sample personal progress as the main visitor experience.
