name: Instructional video direction
description: Approved pilot quality, teaching goals, and the reason for pre-rendered delivery
---
The user wants engaging, AI-generated animated lessons with a teacher/mentor, not silent visual summaries. The complete Community Survey pilot was approved in widescreen 16:9 at High 1080p.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose these pilot settings and asked for video that teaches rather than merely summarizes.

**How to apply:** Keep cognitive reasoning and adaptive support within the viewing journey. Do not treat the approval as permission to generate every course's footage at once.

High-quality editorial pilot videos should be prepared once and stored durably rather than regenerated whenever a student presses play.

**Why:** Generated footage is expensive, slow, and limited to short clips; the longer teaching lesson must combine footage, narration, timed captions and formative interaction reliably.

**How to apply:** Keep playback independent of generation. Clearly distinguish generated footage from an illustrated avatar with composited motion; do not claim lip-sync when it is not present.
