---
name: Student assessment purpose
description: Why Adhyayaan collects learner assessments and how students enter that process.
---
The user requested a psychometric test to inform adaptive learning, AI-enabled custom teaching and career counselling, with a detailed analytical report covering positives, negatives and suggestions saved in the student's profile.

**Why:** The user wants assessment profiling to improve teaching and career guidance, not merely produce a test score.

**How to apply:** Connect reports to the learning journey and career exploration rather than presenting an isolated questionnaire.

The assessment is mandatory after new signup. For existing signed-in users, add an option on the homepage after login to complete it.

**Why:** These are the user's explicit onboarding requirements; existing users were not asked to be locked out.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction between new-account onboarding and the existing-user homepage entry point.
