Use simpler English for Indian audiences. Take inspiration from ASD-STE100 (Simplified Technical English).

**Why:** The user observed that the existing content was too advanced or stylised.

**How to apply:** Prefer short sentences, familiar words, active voice and one action per step. Use a consistent word for each concept. Define necessary technical terms without removing important academic, ethics or safety conditions. For Bengali course content, write in natural Indian Bengali for students: preserve the meaning and rephrase the English structure instead of translating sentence by sentence. Keep common academic terms only when students use them, and explain them in plain Bengali. Do not claim formal ASD-STE100 compliance without review against the standard.
